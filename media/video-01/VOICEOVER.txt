Zero motivation. One hundred percent discipline.

But if your system needs you to hate yourself, it isn't discipline. It is punishment with a productivity label.

Discipline is a standard for your behaviour. Punishment turns every missed action into a verdict on your character.

Useful discipline asks: what made this hard, what can I change, and when will I return?

Put the book where the phone normally goes. Make the first action small. Decide now what you will do after a missed day.

You do not need a softer goal. You need a practice that can survive being human.
